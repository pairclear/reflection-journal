# Before a hard conversation

> If you are afraid of how your partner will react, put your safety first. Preparing better words does not make an unsafe conversation safe. See the box at the bottom of the page.

## What do I want to talk about, in one or two sentences?
_If the talk goes well, what will be different afterwards?_



## What exactly do I want to ask for? A concrete action, not "be more attentive".
_What would a small first version of it look like?_



## What am I afraid of in this conversation?
_If that happened, what would I do?_



## How might my partner see this situation?
_What in their view do I agree with, even a little?_



## Write the opening line, starting from yourself: "I feel ... when ..., and I would like ..."
_Read it aloud. Does it sound like a request or like a charge?_



## When and where is a good moment for it?
_Not in passing, not when someone is tired or hungry, not in front of others._



## What will I do if the conversation starts going badly?
_Which words will I use to ask for a pause, and when will we come back to it?_



## One thought I take away from this.
_One or two sentences._



## One small step, and when I will take it.
_Small enough that it will really happen._
