# When we feel distant

> Distance is common in long relationships and usually builds slowly. The point here is to notice it, not to find who is to blame.

## When did I last feel really close to my partner? What was happening then?
_What made that moment possible?_



## What has changed since then?
_Between us, in the life around us, in me?_



## What do I miss most?
_Is it something specific: time, touch, talking, laughing together?_



## What do I usually do when I feel distant: pull back, reach out, wait?
_What does my partner see of this from the outside?_



## What small step towards contact could I take this week?
_What would make it easy for my partner to respond?_



## Do I want to talk about this distance with my partner?
_How could I start without blame?_



## One thought I take away from this.
_One or two sentences._



## One small step, and when I will take it.
_Small enough that it will really happen._
