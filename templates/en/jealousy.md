# When I feel jealous

> Jealousy is a feeling, not a fact about your partner. If jealousy in your relationship has turned into control or threats, from either side, that is a reason to talk to a specialist.

## What happened that set off the feeling? Facts only.
_What did I see or hear myself, and what did I add in my head?_



## What exactly am I afraid of?
_Losing them? Being replaced? Being deceived? Mattering less?_



## How strong is the feeling right now, from 0 to 10?
_What makes it stronger, and what makes it weaker?_



## What did I do with it: check, ask, stay silent, accuse?
_How did that work for us?_



## What would help me feel more secure in this relationship?
_Is it something to ask for, something I can do myself, or both?_



## Do I want to say something to my partner about it?
_How can I say it as my feeling, not as a charge against them?_



## One thought I take away from this.
_One or two sentences._



## One small step, and when I will take it.
_Small enough that it will really happen._
