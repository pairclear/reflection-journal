# Weekly couple check-in

> If you do it together, each of you writes alone first, then you share only what you want to share. Start with the good parts.

## What was good between us this week?
_What did my partner do that I appreciated, and did I tell them?_



## When did I feel close this week? When did I feel distant?
_What was going on around those moments?_



## Is there something I left unsaid?
_Is it worth bringing up, and how?_



## What do I need from us next week? One concrete request.
_How will I ask for it?_



## What can I give next week? One small thing.
_When exactly will I do it?_



## What are we looking forward to together?
_If nothing comes to mind, what could we plan?_



## One thought I take away from this.
_One or two sentences._



## One small step, and when I will take it.
_Small enough that it will really happen._
