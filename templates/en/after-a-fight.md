# After a fight

> If you are still boiling, take a break first: at least 20 minutes of something else (a walk, a shower, music). Writing goes better once your body has calmed down.

## What happened, as a camera would have recorded it? Only words and actions, no interpretations.
_What was the first moment it started going wrong?_



## What did I feel during the fight, and what do I feel now?
_Which feeling is the hardest to admit?_



## What did I tell myself about my partner in that moment?
_What else could be true? Name one other explanation._



## What did I need in that moment and not get?
_Did I say it out loud, or did I expect it to be guessed?_



## What was my part in how it went? Not blame, just my share.
_What would I do differently in that same moment?_



## What might my partner have felt or needed?
_This is a guess. How could I check it instead of assuming?_



## What do I want now: to talk it through, to apologise, to ask for something, or to let it go?
_What first sentence could I start with?_



## One thought I take away from this.
_One or two sentences._



## One small step, and when I will take it.
_Small enough that it will really happen._
